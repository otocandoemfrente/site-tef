relato:
  - 'During a Tocando em Frente activity about dreams, a student said she didn’t have one. Little by little, through a conversation about her interests, she got involved in the activity and shared that she was a hair braider and loved making braids.'
  - 'In another session, she still felt insecure about presenting in front of the class. Even so, she decided to take part. Together with her classmates, she created Cachinhos de Sonhos (“Dream Curls”), a character who celebrates Afro-Brazilian culture. The activity turned a skill that was already part of her life into a possibility for creation, expression and impact in the community.'
